# Skills for Payroll Professionals
Useful skills, tools, and workflows for payroll Professionals.

## Using our skills

### My org doesn't use Claude, can I use co-pilot or ChatGPT?
Yes, you can still use our skills with CoPilot or ChatGPT, but you may need to just paste in our skill as a prompt into a chat. 

### Will the output always be correct?
AI tools are good at structure and formatting — but they can get details wrong, especially system-specific steps or anything that depends on your organisation's setup. Always review the output before using it with your team, and keep a copy of your original document before you paste it in. The skill is a starting point, not a finished product you can use without checking.
